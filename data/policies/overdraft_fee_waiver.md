# Overdraft Fee Waiver Policy

Policy owner: Retail Banking Operations. Applies to all personal current accounts and SME business accounts held with Nordstern Bank.

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines when a relationship manager (RM) may waive an overdraft fee that was charged to a customer account. Waivers are a goodwill measure. They are not a right of the customer, and they must be applied consistently so that customers in the same situation are treated the same way.

## 2. Eligibility

1. A customer may receive at most 2 overdraft fee waivers in any rolling 12-month period.
2. A waiver is allowed only if the account balance was restored to zero or above within 5 business days of the overdraft.
3. The account must have been open for at least 6 months at the date of the overdraft.
4. Accounts that are in collections, or whose holder has a loan more than 90 days past due, are not eligible.
5. A waiver may not be granted for an overdraft that was caused by a returned or reversed payment flagged under the AML transaction monitoring policy.

## 3. Amount Limits

1. A single waiver covers at most one overdraft fee of up to 25 EUR.
2. An RM may approve waivers up to a total of 50 EUR per customer per rolling 12 months.
3. Any waiver above these limits requires approval by the branch manager.
4. Interest charged on the overdrawn balance is not waived under this policy; only the fixed overdraft fee is.

## 4. Approval and Documentation

1. The RM records every waiver in the customer file with the date, the fee amount and the reason.
2. The reason must be one of: first occurrence, bank error, hardship case, or a documented technical problem with a payment channel.
3. Waivers granted because of a bank error do not count towards the limit of 2 waivers per 12 months.
4. The customer is informed of the waiver in writing within 3 business days.

## 5. Monitoring

1. Retail Banking Operations reviews all waivers each quarter.
2. If an RM's waiver rate is more than 20% above the regional average for 2 consecutive quarters, the RM's waivers are subject to four-eyes approval for the following quarter.
3. Patterns that suggest a customer is repeatedly relying on the overdraft are referred to the financial health team instead of being waived again.
