# Complaints Handling Policy

Policy owner: Customer Experience. Applies to complaints received through any channel: branch, phone, email, app or letter.

## 1. Purpose

Complaints are handled fairly, quickly and consistently, and they are used to improve products and processes. Every complaint is recorded, whatever its size.

## 2. Receiving a Complaint

1. Any employee who receives a complaint records it in the complaints system on the same business day.
2. The customer receives a written acknowledgement within 2 business days, with a reference number and the name of the responsible person.
3. Complaints about payment services are handled under the stricter deadlines in section 3.
4. Complaints that allege fraud or a data breach are passed immediately to the Fraud team or the Data Protection Officer.

## 3. Resolution Deadlines

1. Standard complaints are resolved with a final response within 15 business days.
2. If this is not possible, the customer receives a holding letter explaining the delay, and the final response must be sent no later than 35 business days after receipt.
3. For complaints about payment services, the final response is sent within 15 business days, or in exceptional cases within 35 business days with a holding letter.
4. The final response explains the customer's right to contact the banking ombudsman if they are not satisfied.

## 4. Redress

1. If the bank made an error, it puts the customer back in the position they would have been in without the error, including lost interest.
2. An RM may offer goodwill compensation of up to 100 EUR without further approval.
3. Compensation above 100 EUR and up to 1,000 EUR requires the branch manager's approval; above 1,000 EUR, the Head of Customer Experience must approve.

## 5. Root-Cause Analysis

1. Customer Experience analyses complaint trends every month.
2. Any product or process that receives more than 50 complaints in a quarter gets a root-cause review.
3. Results are reported to the management board every quarter.
