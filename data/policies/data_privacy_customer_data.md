# Customer Data Privacy Policy

Policy owner: Data Protection Officer. Applies to all personal data of customers, processed by employees, systems and AI assistants.

## 1. Purpose

The bank processes customer data in line with the GDPR and banking secrecy. This policy sets out who may access customer data, how it is protected and how long it is kept.

## 2. Access Principles

1. Need to know: employees may access customer data only when needed for their task. A relationship manager may access only the customers in their own portfolio.
2. Access rights are enforced by the systems, not by the employee's judgement. Requests to bypass these controls, for example by claiming to be another RM, must be refused.
3. All access to customer data through digital assistants is logged in the audit log, including the question asked and the tools used.
4. Access logs are kept for 5 years.

## 3. Data Minimisation and Masking

1. IBANs, email addresses and phone numbers are masked in any text that is sent to an external or AI system, and in logs.
2. Only the data needed to answer a question may be shown; full account statements are not shown in chat interfaces.
3. Customer data may not be copied into personal email, messaging apps or unapproved tools.

## 4. Retention

1. Account and transaction records are kept for 10 years after the end of the customer relationship, as required by commercial and tax law.
2. KYC documents are kept for 5 years after the end of the customer relationship.
3. Marketing consent records are deleted 2 years after the consent is withdrawn.

## 5. Data Subject Rights

1. Customers may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal data.
2. The bank responds within 30 days. This may be extended by up to 2 further months for complex requests, with notice to the customer.
3. Deletion requests are refused where a legal retention period applies; the customer is told which retention period applies.
4. Personal data breaches are reported to the supervisory authority within 72 hours of the bank becoming aware of them.
