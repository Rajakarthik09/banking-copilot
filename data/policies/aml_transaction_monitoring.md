# AML Transaction Monitoring Policy

Policy owner: Financial Crime Compliance. Applies to all transactions on all customer accounts.

## 1. Purpose

The bank monitors transactions to detect possible money laundering and terrorist financing. Automated rules flag transactions for review. A flag is not an accusation; it means an analyst must look at the transaction.

## 2. Monitoring Rules

1. AML-01 high-risk country: any payment to or from a counterparty in a high-risk jurisdiction is flagged. The current list is Iran (IR), North Korea (KP), Myanmar (MM), Syria (SY) and Yemen (YE).
2. AML-02 structuring: 3 or more cash deposits by the same customer of between 9,000 and 9,999 EUR within 7 days are flagged, because they suggest splitting amounts to stay below the 10,000 EUR reporting threshold.
3. AML-03 velocity: 5 or more transactions by the same customer within 24 hours, when this is unusual for the customer, are flagged.
4. Rules are reviewed by Financial Crime Compliance at least once every 12 months.

## 3. Alert Handling

1. Every flagged transaction creates an alert that must be reviewed by an AML analyst within 2 business days.
2. The analyst closes the alert as a false positive, or escalates it to an investigation.
3. Investigations must be completed within 20 business days.
4. The RM may be asked for context about the customer but must not tell the customer about the alert or the investigation (no tipping off).

## 4. Reporting

1. If an investigation finds reasonable grounds for suspicion, the bank files a suspicious activity report with the Financial Intelligence Unit within 3 business days of that decision.
2. AML-01 alerts involving sanctioned persons are reported immediately and the payment is frozen.
3. Statistics on alerts, false positive rates and filed reports are reported to the management board every quarter.

## 5. Customer Impact

1. While an AML-01 alert is open, further payments to the same high-risk country require compliance approval.
2. A customer with 3 or more escalated alerts within 12 months is reviewed for a possible exit of the relationship under the account closure policy.
3. Customers with an open AML-01 alert are classified as high risk for KYC purposes.
