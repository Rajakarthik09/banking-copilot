# KYC Refresh Policy

Policy owner: Financial Crime Compliance. Applies to all customers, retail and SME.

## 1. Purpose

Know Your Customer (KYC) information must stay current so that the bank can detect money laundering, sanctions exposure and fraud. This policy sets how often a customer's KYC file must be reviewed and what happens if a review is late.

## 2. Review Frequency

1. High-risk customers must have a full KYC review every 12 months.
2. Standard-risk customers must have a full KYC review every 24 months.
3. Low-risk retail customers must have a full KYC review every 36 months.
4. Unless a customer has been explicitly classified otherwise, the standard frequency of 24 months applies.
5. Any customer with a transaction flagged under AML-01 is reclassified as high risk until the alert is closed.

## 3. Review Content

1. Identity: a valid ID document, checked against the original or through video identification.
2. Address and contact details, including phone number and email.
3. For SME customers: the commercial register extract, the list of beneficial owners holding 25% or more, and the expected business activity.
4. Source of funds for any customer whose yearly incoming payments exceed 100,000 EUR.

## 4. Overdue Reviews

1. The RM receives an overdue notice on the day a review becomes due.
2. If a review is more than 60 days overdue, the customer may not open new products.
3. If a review is more than 180 days overdue, outgoing payments above 1,000 EUR require compliance approval.
4. Overdue reviews are reported to Financial Crime Compliance every week.

## 5. Responsibilities

1. The RM owns the KYC review for every customer in their portfolio and initiates the review at least 30 days before it is due.
2. Financial Crime Compliance performs quality checks on a sample of at least 10% of completed reviews each quarter.
3. Reviews are recorded with the review date, which becomes the new KYC last-review date in the core banking system.
