# Account Closure Policy

Policy owner: Retail Banking Operations. Applies to current accounts and savings accounts of retail and SME customers.

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how accounts are closed, either at the customer's request or by the bank, and how remaining balances are handled.

## 2. Closure by the Customer

1. A customer may close a current account at any time, without a notice period and free of charge.
2. An account may not be closed while it has a negative balance, or while the customer has an outstanding loan that is repaid from that account, unless another repayment account is set up first.
3. Any remaining positive balance is transferred to an account named by the customer within 5 business days.
4. Savings accounts with a fixed term may be closed early only with the early-withdrawal fee defined in the product terms.

## 3. Closure by the Bank

1. The bank may close an account with a notice period of 2 months, stating the reason where the law allows it.
2. The bank may close an account without notice for an important reason, such as serious breach of contract, fraud, or a decision under the AML transaction monitoring policy.
3. Closure decisions for AML reasons are taken by Financial Crime Compliance, not by the RM, and the reason is not given to the customer.

## 4. Dormant Accounts

1. An account with no customer-initiated transaction for 24 months is classified as dormant.
2. The bank contacts the customer of a dormant account in writing at least twice, at least 3 months apart.
3. Dormant account balances are never forfeited; they remain claimable by the customer or their heirs.

## 5. After Closure

1. Direct debits and standing orders are cancelled on the closure date.
2. Incoming payments after closure are returned to the sender within 3 business days.
3. Account records are kept according to the retention periods in the customer data privacy policy.
