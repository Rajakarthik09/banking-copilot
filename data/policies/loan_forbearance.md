# Loan Forbearance Policy

Policy owner: Credit Risk Management. Applies to mortgages, SME term loans and consumer loans.

## 1. Purpose

Forbearance is a concession given to a borrower who is facing, or about to face, financial difficulty. The aim is to bring the borrower back to regular repayment and to avoid default. Forbearance must never be used only to hide arrears.

## 2. Eligibility

1. The borrower must show evidence of temporary financial hardship, such as loss of income, illness, or a documented drop in business revenue of at least 30%.
2. The loan must be no more than 90 days past due at the time of the request. Loans more than 90 days past due are handled by the Workout team, not by the RM.
3. A borrower may receive at most 2 forbearance measures within any 5-year period.
4. The borrower must have made at least 6 regular monthly payments since the loan was disbursed.

## 3. Available Measures

1. Payment holiday: full suspension of instalments for up to 6 months. Interest continues to accrue.
2. Reduced instalments: instalments reduced by up to 50% for up to 12 months.
3. Term extension: the remaining term may be extended by up to 5 years, but never beyond the borrower's 75th birthday for consumer loans and mortgages.
4. Measures may not be combined, except that a term extension may follow a payment holiday.

## 4. Approval

1. An RM may approve a payment holiday of up to 3 months on loans with an outstanding balance of up to 250,000 EUR.
2. All other measures, and any loan in risk tier D, require approval by the credit committee.
3. The credit committee decides within 10 business days of receiving a complete request.

## 5. Classification and Reporting

1. A loan that receives forbearance is marked as forborne for a probation period of at least 24 months.
2. During the probation period, if the loan becomes more than 30 days past due again, it is reclassified as non-performing.
3. Credit Risk Management reports the number and total outstanding balance of forborne loans to the board every month.
4. Forbearance does not by itself change the loan's risk tier; tiers are assigned under the SME credit risk tier policy and the retail scoring model.
